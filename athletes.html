Athletes & Juice Plus+

Fueled by plants. Trusted by athletes.

From Olympic gold medalists and world champions to NFL veterans and IRONMAN competitors, athletes across many sports have publicly described using Juice Plus+ as part of their nutrition routines. Several have also represented the brand as ambassadors or partners.

Every claim on this page links to its source. Each card shows what we can document about that athlete's connection to Juice Plus+, and whether it is current or historical. Some athletes listed are or were paid ambassadors or independent Juice Plus+ Partners. An athlete using a product is a personal testimonial, not scientific evidence that it improves performance.

Beyond individual athletes

Organization & community partnerships

Juice Plus+ has also partnered with sports organizations and charitable events. These are agreements with the organization, not endorsements by every athlete connected to it.

Governing body · Historical

USA Volleyball

April 2019 – March 2021

USA Volleyball, the sport's national governing body, announced Juice Plus+ as its exclusive nutritional supplement partner in a two-year agreement running through March 31, 2021.

Covers the organization's sponsorship only; it does not mean individual USA Volleyball athletes endorsed Juice Plus+.

View source
Training center · 2024

United States Performance Center

Charlotte, NC · Announced 2024

Juice Plus+ became an official sponsor of the USPC. Its Fueling Station serves Juice Plus+ Essentials Capsules (Fruit, Vegetable and Berry Blends) and Omega Blend to athletes who train there.

Products are made available to athletes training at the center; this does not mean every athlete there uses or endorses them.

Olympic team · Historical

German Olympic Team (DOSB)

Through London 2012

NSA AG, then the European distributor of Juice Plus+, was a licensed partner of the German Olympic Sports Confederation and was named exclusive nutrition partner of the German Olympic team through the London 2012 Games.

A historical commercial partnership with the confederation, not individual athlete endorsements.

Charity event · Current

St. Jude Memphis Marathon Weekend

Partner since 2001 · Presenting sponsor

The Juice Plus+ Company and the Juice Plus+ Foundation are the presenting sponsor of St. Jude Memphis Marathon Weekend, the largest single-day fundraising event for St. Jude Children's Research Hospital. The company has supported the event since 2001 and marked a 24-year commitment in 2025.

A corporate charitable sponsorship benefiting St. Jude; it is not a St. Jude endorsement of Juice Plus+ products.
