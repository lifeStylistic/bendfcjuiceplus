Your questions, answered

Healthy Starts FAQ

Who is eligible for Healthy Starts?
An adult 18+ subscribes to a qualifying Juice Plus+ product, then gifts free Fruit & Vegetable Blends to a young person aged 4–25.
What does it cost?
The young person's product is free only when a qualifying adult purchases their own Healthy Starts subscription. With that adult subscription in place, families pay just a $12 handling fee every four months per shipment for the young person's product.
How often does product ship?
Free product ships every 4 months, and everything (adult and child) ships to one address for convenience.
How does Bend FC benefit?
5% of every qualifying order goes back to Bend FC to fund scholarships, player development, fields and club growth, renewing year after year.
Is it backed by research?
Yes. Juice Plus+ is among the most thoroughly researched products of its kind, with 40+ peer-reviewed publications across 40+ universities and medical centers.
