## How this was researched — read this first

The session's network policy **blocked direct page fetches** for every source site tried (juiceplus.com, us.juiceplus.com, prnewswire.com, usavolleyball.org, medium.com, wikipedia.org, web.archive.org). Research was done through web search, which returns result titles, URLs and the search engine's extracts of each page. So:

* Every source URL below appeared in real search results (none were typed from memory).
* The *content* attributed to each source comes from the search engine's extract, **not from reading the page directly**. Wording, dates and whether each link still works need a direct read before publishing (see §D).
* Confidence ratings below assume the extracts are accurate. Treat "HIGH" as "HIGH, pending direct read".

## D. Claim audit — items needing verification before publishing

**Blocking (must be checked by reading the page directly):**

1. **All Juice Plus+ relationship wording.** Confirm each card's "connection" sentence against the actual source page, especially Vinecki ("since she was a child"), Grothe ("since he was 10"), Simmons ("takes … daily"), and Hochevar's listing on the Juice Plus+ athletes page.
2. **Link health.** Several Juice Plus+ URLs are from the old `juiceplus.com/us/en/...` site; the company has moved to `us.juiceplus.com`. Old links may redirect or 404. Must test every link.
3. **Shannon Miller** — only secondary evidence (speaker bios, a Juice Plus+ Facebook post). Ideally confirm on shannonmiller.com or a Juice Plus+ page, or drop to "has represented…" wording as used. Also note Wikipedia's "2015 business partnership… launching a branded line" line looked garbled and was **not** used.
4. **Jason Fowler** — interview publication date unknown; titles 2009 & 2016 come from search extracts. A Business Wire headline says he won the "2012 Ironman World Championship", which one extract attributed to the 70.3 Worlds. That claim was removed; confirm title years with IRONMAN results.
5. **Ana Ivanović** — Tennis Foodie article date unknown, so "was not then a paid ambassador" is time-bound; the card wording reflects that.
6. **Errani Juice Plus+ start date** — one extract said Sept 2023; not used on the page. The 2025 and 2026 renewals are what the card relies on.
7. **USPC press release date** — shown as "2024"; Direct Selling News coverage is dated 31 Jul 2024.

**Claims deliberately avoided (fail the audit):**

* "Most decorated U.S. gymnast in Olympic history" for Shannon Miller — no longer true (Simone Biles has since surpassed her). Not used.
* USPC director quote saying Juice Plus+ products are "enhancing their performance and recovery" — a performance claim. Not quoted.
* Zane Grothe's quote that he "would not be where he is today… had he not had Juice Plus+" — implies causation. Not quoted.
* Any statement that athletes use Juice Plus+ "because it improves performance". Not used.

**Disclosures included:** Proehl is an independent Juice Plus+ Partner (financial interest); Errani and others are or were paid ambassadors; organization deals are not athlete endorsements; no athlete photos used.

**Images:** none used. No athlete photography has confirmed reuse rights.
